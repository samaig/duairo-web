How It Works

Healthcare in Four Steps

Click on the steps to see how Duairo transforms your journey from symptom to recovery.

1

Symptom Collection

Describe your health concerns in natural language. Our system captures vital data including temperature, heart rate, photos, and videos to map severity and patterns for a complete clinical picture.

2

Smart Clinical Analysis

Fine-tuned with advice from NHS GPs, our AI conducts a medical-grade consultation by asking targeted safety questions to mirror a physical doctor's visit and delivering instant assessment.

3

Actionable Plan & Summary

Receive clear next steps from self-care to urgent escalation. We generate a structured clinical summary detailing symptoms and reasoning that is ready to be shared with any healthcare provider.

4

Track & Monitor

Healthcare doesn't stop at diagnosis. Securely track your history, monitor symptom patterns over time, and receive intelligent follow-up reminders to ensure your recovery stays on track.

For Healthcare Partners

Supporting Healthcare Systems

Reducing clinical workload and ensuring equitable access through intelligent infrastructure.

Seamless Clinical Dashboard

We provide a specialized Front-End CMS that connects seamlessly with your existing systems (EMR/EHR). Our clinician dashboard presents pre-sorted symptom summaries and risk analysis before the patient enters the room. This eliminates administrative data entry, giving doctors time back to focus on care.

Scalable Triage for Any Region

Whether serving remote locations or densely populated urban centers, Duairo reduces pressure on hospitals. Our AI assesses symptom severity instantly and faster than transporting a patient to a GP. This reduces wait times, prevents unnecessary visits, and makes high-quality triage scalable worldwide.

Unbiased, Equitable Diagnosis

Patients often face underdiagnosis or misdiagnosis based on race and gender. Duairo is built to prevent this. By utilizing objective data collection and standardized clinical protocols, we ensure every patient receives an accurate, evidence-based assessment regardless of their background or demographic.
