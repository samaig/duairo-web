How Duairo Works

From initial consultation to ongoing care — a seamless, patient-led healthcare experience guided by medical excellence

Share Your Symptoms

Describe your health concerns in natural conversation. Our platform asks targeted questions just like a GP would, building a complete picture of your health.

Advanced clinical intelligence analyzes your symptoms with medical-grade accuracy, following NHS guidelines and best practices.

03

Receive Guidance

Get evidence-based health guidance with clear next steps, risk assessment, and exportable summaries for your GP.

Our Mission

Transforming Healthcare Access

Duairo combines advanced technology with medical expertise to deliver safe, accessible, and personalised healthcare. Our mission is to empower patients to take charge of their health while supporting clinicians and healthcare systems to work smarter, not harder.
