How It Works

Simple, Secure, Intelligent

From account creation to personalized care, Duairo makes healthcare accessible, safe, and tailored to you.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up securely with encrypted authentication. Your profile becomes the foundation for personalized, safe healthcare access.

2

Describe Your Symptoms

Share your health concerns in natural language. Our intelligent system identifies symptoms, severity, and patterns instantly.

3

Smart Analysis

Advanced clinical intelligence asks targeted follow-up questions, evaluates risk in real-time, and provides evidence-based guidance.

4

Clear Recommendations

Receive straightforward next steps: self-care guidance, pharmacy options, GP advice, or urgent care escalation when needed.

5

Structured Summary

Get a complete medical-style summary including symptoms, severity, reasoning, and recommendations ready to share with clinicians.

6

Track & Monitor

Access your secure chat history, spot patterns over time, and receive helpful follow-up reminders and health insights.
